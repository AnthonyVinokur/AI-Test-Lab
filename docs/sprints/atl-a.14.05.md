# ATL-A.14.05 — Cross-Attestation Binding

Requires the approval to name the verified decision and requires exact agreement on artifact identity, artifact digest, tenant, environment, release, scope, and ordered preserved conditions. The artifact identity comes from ATL-A.12's verified `system_id`; the approval remains transitively bound through the exact decision-attestation ID. Any decision, approval, artifact, tenant, environment, release, scope, or condition substitution fails closed.
