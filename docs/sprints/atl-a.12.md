# ATL-A.12 — Evidence-Based Policy Decision and Quality-Gate Attestation

ATL-A.12 adds a deterministic, fail-closed decision boundary over evidence admitted through ATL-A.01–ATL-A.11. It binds a frozen policy version and its canonical digest to exact trusted evidence, requirement outcomes, artifact, tenant, environment, decision time, expiration, and authority.

Public operators cover equality, inequality, thresholds, set membership, presence, absence, and freshness. Missing, conflicting, stale, untrusted, or uninterpretable mandatory evidence can never silently pass. Signed attestations are independently verifiable; public outcomes expose stable reasons without evidence values, policy parameters, signatures, or proprietary scoring logic.
